# Project description — English

LectureBridge AI helps deaf and hard-of-hearing students recover the structure and meaning of recorded lectures instead of navigating captions alone. The platform converts a timestamped source transcript into evidence-linked questions, answers, examples, topic changes, and important moments. Students can use a Semantic Timeline, ask what they missed during a recent interval, or ask a question about the current lecture. Answers remain restricted to lecture evidence, cite backend-verified timestamps, and abstain when support is insufficient. Summaries, highlights, flashcards, and quizzes reuse the same source identifiers. Instructors and administrators can confirm, correct, reject, and relink AI output through an auditable review workflow. The evaluation package distinguishes synthetic engineering fixtures from model-quality evidence and reports blockers instead of invented scores. LectureBridge focuses on transparent, accessible, and reviewable assistance for inclusive learning while preserving protected media, authorization boundaries, source traceability, and human judgment throughout the lecture workflow.
