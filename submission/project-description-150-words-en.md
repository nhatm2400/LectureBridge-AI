# Submission description — English

**Project name:** LectureBridge AI

**Description (maximum 150 words):**

LectureBridge AI helps learners—including Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing students who choose text-first tools—recover lecture structure that captions alone may not reveal. It converts timestamped VI, EN, and code-switch transcripts into source-linked topic changes, questions, answers, examples, and important moments. Learners can navigate a Semantic Timeline, recover recent context, and ask current-lecture questions; supported answers cite backend-validated evidence, while unsupported questions abstain. AI is essential for mapping long multilingual transcripts into reusable semantic structure at lecture scale. The same evidence graph supports summaries, highlights, flashcards, and quizzes, with human review and auditable provenance. In a small human-verified synthetic evaluation, Event prediction precision was 20/20, full-recall VI/EN Event recall was 11/12, Q↔A linking was 3/3, Context grounding was 51/51, and unsupported-question abstention was 5/5. Results are model-specific and do not establish universal accessibility effectiveness.
